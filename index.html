About the CMIP6 Climate Projections

This interactive visualization tool explores projected climate changes across the United States using data generated from the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project Phase 6 (CMIP6). These models help scientists understand future climate dynamics under various socioeconomic pathways.

Emissions Scenarios (Shared Socioeconomic Pathways - SSPs)

  • SSP1-2.6 (Low Emissions): A sustainable pathway where global carbon emissions cut sharply, keeping warming close to or under 2°C.
  • SSP2-4.5 (Moderate Emissions): A middle-of-the-road scenario where historical emissions patterns continue with modest reduction policies.
  • SSP3-7.0 (High Emissions): Regional rivalry and high energy demand with slower economic growth and limited decarbonization.
  • SSP5-8.5 (High Emissions / Fossil-fueled Development): A worst-case baseline scenario characterized by heavily fossil-fueled economies and rapid temperature expansion.

Methodology

Temperature values are calculated as warming anomalies relative to a historical baseline, showing clear spatial variations across different regions of the continental United States.
